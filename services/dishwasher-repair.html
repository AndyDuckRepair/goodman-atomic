Common dishwasher problems we fix

Dishes come out dirty or gritty

Food residue, grease, or spots remain after the cycle. The spray arms may be clogged, the wash pump may be weak, or the water temperature may not be reaching the right level.

Water won't drain from the tub

Standing water remains after the cycle ends. This usually points to a clogged drain filter, a failed drain pump, or a blocked drain hose connection.

Water leaking onto the floor

Puddles form in front of or under the machine. Common causes include a worn door gasket, a cracked door latch assembly, a faulty float switch, or a loose inlet valve connection.

Won't start or fill with water

The control panel lights up but no cycle begins, or the machine runs but no water enters. Could be a defective door latch, failed water inlet valve, or a blown thermal fuse.

Top rack not cleaning properly

Glasses and cups on the upper level come out with residue while the bottom rack is clean. Usually caused by a broken or clogged upper spray arm or low water pressure.

Persistent odors or cloudy glassware

A foul smell lingers between cycles, or glassware develops a hazy film. Often a blocked filter that hasn't been cleaned, a faulty heating element, or hard water build-up in the sump.
