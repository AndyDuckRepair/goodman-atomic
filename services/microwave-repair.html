Common microwave problems we fix

Runs but doesn't heat food

The timer counts down and the light works, but food stays cold. The magnetron, high-voltage diode, or capacitor is likely the culprit — these are the components that generate microwave energy.

Sparking or arcing inside the cavity

Visible flashing during operation. If the waveguide cover is not the cause, the issue may be with a damaged interior surface, a faulty stirrer blade, or the turntable motor area.

Turntable not spinning

Food heats unevenly because the glass tray sits still during the cycle. Usually caused by a failed turntable motor, a worn coupler, or a damaged ring assembly under the tray.

Display or controls not responding

Buttons require multiple presses, random functions activate, or the panel is completely dead. This often points to a failed touchpad membrane, a faulty control board, or a blown main fuse.

Unusual noises during operation

Loud buzzing, grinding, or rattling beyond the normal hum. A failing magnetron has a distinct loud buzz; a grinding sound usually means the turntable motor or cooling fan is worn.

Door won't latch or won't stop when opened

The door fails to close securely, or the microwave keeps running when the door is open — a safety fault. The door switches, latch hooks, or latch spring require immediate inspection.
