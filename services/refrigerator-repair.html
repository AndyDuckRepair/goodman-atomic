Common refrigerator problems we fix

Not cooling or not cold enough

Food spoils too quickly, temp readings are off. Usually a refrigerant, compressor, or evaporator issue.

Water leaking on the floor

Pooling under or inside the unit. Often a clogged drain line, failing water inlet valve, or cracked drain pan.

Excessive frost or ice buildup

Frost covering the freezer walls or food. Usually a defrost heater, thermostat, or timer has failed.

Ice maker not working

No ice, small cubes, or cubes that stick together. We service the ice maker unit, water line and fill valve.

Unusual noises

Banging, buzzing, clicking, or rattling. Could be the condenser fan, evaporator fan, or compressor.

Runs constantly or cycles too fast

The compressor never rests, raising your electricity bill. Often caused by dirty coils, a faulty thermostat, or a door seal failure.
