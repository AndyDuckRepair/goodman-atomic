What we repair

Every machine that keeps a house running

  • Refrigerators and freezers

    Not cooling, leaking, frosting over, or running loud.

  • Washers

    Will not drain, will not spin, shakes, or leaks on the floor.

  • Dryers

    No heat, long dry times, a drum that will not turn.

  • Dishwashers

    Dishes come out dirty, water stays in the tub, the door leaks.

  • Ovens and stoves

    Burners that will not light, uneven baking, a dead igniter.

  • Microwaves

    Runs but does not heat, sparks, or a turntable that stopped.

We also repair wine coolers and ice machines, at home or in a commercial kitchen.

How a repair goes

Three steps from broken to running

  1. Tell us what broke

    Book in the chat terminal, call, or send the request form. Pick the arrival window that suits you.

  2. Get a clear estimate

    A technician finds the fault and gives you the cost upfront. Work starts only after you agree.

  3. Repaired and covered

    We fix the appliance and back the repair with a warranty of 3 to 12 months.
